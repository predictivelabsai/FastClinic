## What FastClinic is — the operations cockpit

![Overview](img/01-overview.png)

**FastClinic** runs the back office of a **multi-specialty clinic** — general
practice, surgical specialties (orthopaedics, ophthalmology, ENT, general surgery,
gynaecology, urology and more) and dental care — in one workspace. The **Overview**
is your daily scorecard: active and total patients, recent visits, revenue and
trends, all dated so you always know how fresh the picture is.

Everything the clinic runs on is here: the **case mix and revenue** across every
department, **appointments** booked into real availability, **billing** with a
balanced ledger, and **patient recall**. It's built for the whole operations team
— reception, practice managers, and finance — not just clinicians.

---

## Sign in & the layout

![Sign in](img/00-login.png)

Open **fastclinic.example** and sign in with your clinic login. The screen then
has **three panes** that stay consistent everywhere:

- **Left** — navigation, grouped by job: *Overview · Operations · Recall &
  Scheduling · Communications · Admin*.
- **Centre** — the workspace: lists, tables, charts and forms.
- **Right** — the **AI Copilot**, always on hand to answer questions about your
  clinic. It can be collapsed to give the workspace more room.

Everything is server-rendered and fast; if you can read a list and press a button,
you can run the clinic from here.

## The AI Copilot — just ask

![AI Copilot](img/02-copilot.png)

The **Copilot** on the right turns plain questions into answers from your live
data. Ask *"Which specialties bring in the most revenue?"*, *"How many surgical
cases this month?"* or *"Which patients are due for recall?"* and it replies with a
ready-to-read table.

Power users can type **slash-commands** for instant results — `/kpi`, `/due`,
`/lapsed`, `/followup`, `/revenue`, `/patient 1206`. The suggested questions under
the box are a good starting point.

## Treatments & Specialties — the case-mix lens

![Treatments & Specialties](img/18-treatments.png)

This is the operations heart of the platform. Every treatment is classified on two
axes — *what* it is (consultation, **surgery**, **dental**, diagnostic,
procedure…) and *which specialty* delivered it — so you can see the whole clinic at
a glance: **revenue and case volume across every department**, surgical
throughput, and the **top procedures** driving income.

Use it to understand where the work and the money are — which specialties are
growing, how much surgical and dental activity you're doing, and which procedures
matter most. It's the view a practice manager opens first.

## Appointments & booking

![Appointments](img/07-appointments.png)

Book patients into **real availability**. Pick a clinician and day and the
schedule shows every slot, free or taken. Booking checks for conflicts, so a
clinician can **never be double-booked**; the same time on another clinician is
still free. Confirm or cancel with one click, and every booking automatically
queues a reminder the day before.

## Billing & payments

![Billing](img/12-billing.png)

Raise **fee invoices** against consultations — from a routine GP visit to a
high-value surgical case — record **part or full payments**, and watch the numbers
stay honest: a live **balanced ledger** underneath means every pound billed and
collected is accounted for. Because the payer is tracked separately from the
patient, a parent's invoice for a child — or an insurer's — is handled naturally.

---

## Recalls Due

![Recalls Due](img/03-reminders.png)

Recall keeps patients on track. This engine surfaces **exactly who is due or
overdue** for a recurring service — childhood and adult immunisations, annual
health checks, and repeat-prescription reviews — ranked most-overdue first.

Recall intervals are **age- and cohort-aware**: a flu booster for an over-65, a
childhood immunisation, and a medication review each follow their own schedule.
Each row comes with a drafted message, ready to send or export.

---

## Lapsed Patients

![Lapsed Patients](img/04-lapsed.png)

Good patients drift away quietly. **Lapsed Patients** lists everyone with no visit
in the last *N* months, ranked by lifetime value so you re-engage the most
valuable relationships first. Adjust the window (6 / 12 / 24 months) to widen or
tighten the net.

---

## Post-Visit Follow-up

![Post-Visit Follow-up](img/05-followup.png)

Care doesn't end when the patient leaves. **Post-Visit Follow-up** lists recent
visits — including post-operative reviews — so you can check on recovery, prompt a
review, or rebook within a window you choose (7 / 14 / 30 / 60 days).

---

## The Recall Loop — queue, send, measure

![Recall Loop](img/06-loop.png)

This closes the loop. **Queue due reminders** turns the recall lists into tracked
reminders. Every message you then send is **logged** — sent, failed, or **blocked
because the patient opted out** — so nobody is contacted twice and consent is
always respected. The scorecard tracks pending reminders, messages sent, blocked
contacts, and the **return rate**: how many contacted patients came back within 30
days.

---

## SMS & Email

![SMS](img/13-sms.png)

Send reminders through your SMS or email provider. Paste a drafted message from any
recall list, or write your own. Opted-out patients are **automatically suppressed**
at the point of sending, so a message never reaches someone who has asked not to be
contacted.

## The weekly playbook

**A simple weekly rhythm keeps the clinic running well:**

1. **See.** Open the Overview, then **Treatments & Specialties** for the week's
   case mix and revenue by department.
2. **Schedule.** Work the Appointments board — fill slots, confirm bookings, keep
   surgical lists moving.
3. **Bill.** Raise invoices for the week's work and record payments; the ledger
   keeps itself balanced.
4. **Recall.** Queue due reminders on the Recall Loop, review the drafted messages,
   and send — opted-out patients are handled for you.
5. **Measure.** Check the Recall Loop's **return rate** and the Overview's active-
   patient and revenue trends.

> **The one question FastClinic answers every day:** *"How is the clinic running
> across every specialty — and what needs attention today?"*
